Our Story · Owner Operated

Somersworth's owner-operated painting company

T&M Painting has been painting homes and businesses around Somersworth since 2012. We are a family-owned shop, and the work stays in the family: the owner personally handles every project, from the first walkthrough to the last piece of tape coming off the trim. Nothing gets handed to a subcontractor.

That means the person who gives you a price is the person on the ladder, with more than 30 years of combined experience behind the brush. You get one point of contact, a straight answer about what your job actually needs, and a crew that treats your house the way it would treat its own. Senior and veteran discounts are always available; just mention it when you call.

Property Management

Painting built around your turnover schedule

Vacancy costs more than paint does. We work to the date the unit has to be back on the market, keep colors consistent across a portfolio, and give you one person to call instead of a dispatcher.

What we handle

  • Unit Turnover PaintingRepainting between tenants to get units rent-ready quickly.
  • Interior PaintingWalls, ceilings, trim, doors, closets, kitchens, bedrooms, and more.
  • Touch-Ups & Maintenance PaintingScuffs, stains, patched walls, and routine repainting.
  • Common Area PaintingHallways, stairwells, lobbies, laundry rooms, leasing offices, and amenities.
  • Exterior PaintingSiding, trim, doors, railings, garages, fences, and other exterior surfaces.
  • Drywall Repair & PatchingNail holes, dents, cracks, and minor damage before painting.
  • Cabinet, Door & Trim PaintingRefresh frequently used surfaces without replacing them.
  • Multi-Unit / Portfolio RepaintingCoordinated painting across multiple properties with consistent colors and finishes.
  • Occupied-Unit PaintingScheduled around residents with protection and minimal disruption.
  • Move-In / Move-Out RefreshesPainting and minor repairs as part of a broader turnover process.
